## Product Description
AltiusNxt's AI-Powered Product Data Enrichment Service helps industrial distributors transform incomplete, inconsistent supplier data into structured, search-ready product content that drives digital growth. Built for complex B2B catalogs in MRO, HVAC, electrical, and plumbing, the service uses AI-driven enrichment with human validation to normalize product information, classify SKUs into the right taxonomy, map attributes, and create clean structured data for PIM, ERP, and eCommerce platforms such as Magento and Shopify.

The result is more than a cleaner catalog. It is a faster, more scalable product onboarding engine that improves discoverability, strengthens faceted search and filtering, and gives buyers the technical detail they need to make confident purchasing decisions. For eCommerce managers and digital transformation leaders, this means less manual data wrangling, fewer catalog bottlenecks, and a more consistent customer experience across channels. For industrial distributors, it creates a stronger foundation for conversion growth, catalog efficiency, and competitive advantage. Instead of treating product data as a back-office task, AltiusNxt turns it into a revenue-enabling asset.
