# AltiusNxt Paid Search Campaign Draft

## Campaign Goal
Generate qualified B2B leads for AltiusNxt's AI-Powered Product Data Enrichment Service from high-intent industrial eCommerce and product data searches.

## Primary Conversion
Request a Catalog Audit

## Secondary Conversion
Talk to an Expert

## Recommended Campaign Theme
Use paid search to capture buyers already looking for industrial product data support, catalog enrichment, taxonomy improvement, and product onboarding acceleration. Keep the message tightly aligned to industrial distributors and complex technical catalogs.

## Core Audience
- Industrial distributors
- eCommerce managers
- PIM and product content teams
- Procurement and supply chain leaders
- Digital transformation leaders in B2B commerce

## Value Proposition For Ads
Turn fragmented industrial product data into structured, searchable, platform-ready content that improves discoverability, reduces manual effort, and speeds up product onboarding.

## Landing Page Message Match
Use the following message pattern on the landing page tied to search traffic:

**Headline:** Turn Incomplete Industrial Product Data Into Conversion-Ready Catalog Content

**Subheadline:** AltiusNxt helps industrial distributors enrich, normalize, classify, and structure technical product data for faster onboarding, stronger discoverability, and better digital buying experiences.

## Recommended Launch Priorities
1. Launch Ad Group 1 first because it has the strongest service-intent alignment.
2. Add Ad Group 2 to capture buyers focused on taxonomy and attribute problems.
3. Add Ad Group 3 once landing page and sales follow-up are ready to speak to platform integration and onboarding speed.

## Optimization Notes
- Start with phrase and exact match for better lead quality.
- Keep landing page copy tightly aligned with each ad group's search intent.
- Route all conversions to a short audit form, not a long demo request form.
- Track form fills, qualified calls, and pipeline contribution by ad group.
- Add industry qualifiers to ads and keywords to reduce generic traffic.
