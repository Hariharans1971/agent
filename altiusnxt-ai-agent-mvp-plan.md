# AltiusNxt AI Agent MVP Plan

## Recommended MVP Name
AltiusNxt Team Copilot

## What Should Launch First
Launch one internal app with two guided workspaces:
- Marketing Copilot
- HR Copilot

This is the fastest path to internal adoption because it gives each team a clear entry point while sharing the same company knowledge base and admin controls.

## What The First Version Should Actually Do

### Marketing Copilot
- generate website and landing page copy
- create LinkedIn posts and email drafts
- produce paid search ad copy
- turn case studies into sales and marketing assets
- generate campaign briefs
- reuse approved messaging and differentiators

### HR Copilot
- draft job descriptions
- summarize resumes
- prepare interview questions
- draft candidate outreach emails
- create onboarding checklists
- answer policy questions from approved HR documents

## Must-Have Data Inputs
- approved company profile
- service pages and case studies
- brand tone guide
- marketing templates
- HR policies
- job descriptions
- onboarding documents
- interview scorecards

## Must-Have Product Controls
- role-based access for HR-sensitive content
- source-grounded responses
- human review before send or publish
- prompt and output logging
- template administration

## What To Avoid In V1
- direct ATS write-back
- autonomous candidate rejection
- autonomous publishing to social channels
- broad company-wide assistant for every department
- deep workflow automation before the prompts and knowledge base are stable

## Best Rollout Sequence
1. Load website content and approved service messaging.
2. Add top six marketing templates and top six HR templates.
3. Pilot with one marketing lead and one HR lead.
4. Collect the most repeated requests and turn them into saved workflows.
5. Add internal documents and refine permissions.

## Success Criteria For First 30 Days
- marketing team uses the agent weekly for content production
- HR team uses the agent weekly for hiring and onboarding support
- users report meaningful time savings on repetitive drafting tasks
- outputs require only light editing before use
- no major trust or permissions issues appear during pilot

## Production Recommendation
Once the prototype is approved, move to a production build with:
- secure authentication
- role-based permissions
- company knowledge ingestion
- document upload
- audit logs
- feedback tracking
- admin controls for templates and sources

## Best Immediate Next Step
Review the prototype structure, then refine the top workflows for:
- landing page generation
- LinkedIn post generation
- job description drafting
- resume summarization

Those four workflows will create the strongest early internal value for AltiusNxt.
